# Platform Overview

SmartAgents is the execution platform layer for LondonoSolutions intelligence systems.
It is the workspace where governed intelligence is translated into operational action.

## Multi-agent orchestration
SmartAgents coordinates specialized agents that collaborate across tasks.
This enables modular capability while preserving policy boundaries and control.

## Research agents
Research agents gather, structure, and summarize information for decision support.
They improve analysis throughput while maintaining traceable outputs.

## Workflow automation
SmartAgents automates recurring operational flows once human intent is approved.
Automation is governed, observable, and aligned with defined decision rights.

## Operational memory
The platform preserves context, state, and relevant history across workflows.
This supports continuity, accountability, and improved future decisions.

## Mini-apps and tools
SmartAgents supports lightweight tools and mini-apps that operational teams can use directly.
These interfaces connect decision logic to day-to-day execution without unnecessary complexity.

## Platform role in the LondonoSolutions stack
SmartAgents is where LondonoSolutions intelligence systems run in practice:
AI analysis upstream, human authority at decision points, and governed execution downstream.
