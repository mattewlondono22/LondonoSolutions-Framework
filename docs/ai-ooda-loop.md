# AI OODA Loop

LondonoSolutions applies a governed OODA loop to decision systems.
The objective is not autonomous control.
The objective is continuous, accountable decision quality.

## Observe
Collect structured signals from operations, market conditions, and internal performance.
AI supports signal detection and surfacing.
Governance ensures data quality and policy-compliant intake.

## Orient
Transform raw signals into context.
AI helps summarize patterns, frame scenarios, and identify dependencies.
Human leaders interpret significance based on mission, risk, and constraints.

## Decide
Decision rights remain with accountable humans.
AI presents options and tradeoffs.
Human authority selects direction, sets intent, and approves action boundaries.

## Act
Systems execute approved actions through governed workflows, agents, and tools.
Execution is policy-aware, monitored, and bounded by defined controls.

## Learn
Outcomes are captured, reviewed, and fed back into models, playbooks, and decision records.
The loop strengthens over time through measured feedback, not assumption.

## Continuous control loop

LondonoSolutions systems combine:
- AI analysis for speed and signal amplification
- Human authority for judgment and accountability
- Governed execution for reliable operational follow-through

This creates a continuous control loop that improves discernment while preserving trust.
