# System Overview

## What intelligence stewardship means

Intelligence stewardship is the practice of governing how insight is produced, interpreted, and applied.
It is not only about generating analysis.
It is about ensuring analysis remains reliable, decisions remain accountable, and actions remain aligned to mission.

## Why organizations need governed AI systems

Most organizations now have access to powerful models.
Few have durable decision systems.
Without governance, AI can accelerate inconsistency, create hidden risk, and weaken institutional trust.
Governed AI systems provide consistency, auditability, and decision continuity across teams, tools, and leadership cycles.

## Five-layer architecture

### 1) Thinking Primitives
Core concepts, shared definitions, and reasoning patterns.
These create a common language for interpretation and reduce ambiguity.

### 2) Foundation
Identity, policy boundaries, data controls, and model access.
This layer establishes trust, security, and controlled operating conditions.

### 3) Analysis
AI-supported synthesis, pattern detection, scenario framing, and option generation.
This layer improves speed and quality of understanding.

### 4) Decision
Human authority for judgment, prioritization, and approval.
This is where accountability is explicit and ownership is assigned.

### 5) Execution
Governed workflows, agents, and tools that carry decisions into operations.
This layer ensures intent is translated into repeatable action.

## Human judgment stays in command

LondonoSolutions systems are designed so human authority remains the control point.
AI contributes intelligence.
People make decisions.
Systems execute under policy and oversight.
