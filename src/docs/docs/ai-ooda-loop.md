## What Makes the LondonoSolutions Loop Different

Typical AI systems collapse sensing, analysis, decision-making, and execution into one opaque system.  
LondonoSolutions separates authority and capability into five explicit layers:

1. **Observe → Knowledge**  
   Signals from the real world are captured, structured, and retained as organizational memory.
2. **Orient → Analyst Agents**  
   Analyst Agents analyze information, recognize patterns, and evaluate scenarios without holding decision authority.
3. **Decide → Human Authority**  
   Human operators apply policy, judgment, and approval logic before any meaningful action is taken.
4. **Act → Execution Systems**  
   Governed tools, APIs, workflows, and automation systems execute approved actions.
5. **Learn → Institutional Memory**  
   Outcomes are recorded so the system improves without losing traceability or human control.

This is cybernetic governance for AI: intelligence acceleration without surrendering authority.
