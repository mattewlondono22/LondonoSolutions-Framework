# The LondonoSolutions Intelligence Standard

**Date:** March 7, 2026  
**Owner:** LondonoSolutions  
**Version:** v1.0

The LondonoSolutions Intelligence Standard defines the minimum operating requirements for governed intelligence systems.

## Standards

### LS-01 Human Authority

AI systems may analyze and recommend, but humans retain final decision authority.

### LS-02 Model Replaceability

No intelligence workflow may depend on a single model vendor.

### LS-03 Reversibility

Every automated action must support rollback.

### LS-04 Auditability

Decisions and reasoning must be traceable.

### LS-05 Institutional Memory

Systems must preserve decision context and outcomes for future learning.
