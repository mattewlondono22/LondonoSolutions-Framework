## Purpose of Our Offerings

LondonoSolutions offerings exist to operationalize intelligence stewardship. We do not sell AI tools or automation in isolation. We design, implement, and govern systems that preserve decision continuity at scale.

Every offering:

- Increases clarity or leverage
- Preserves human authority
- Is auditable and reversible
- Can be safely upgraded without loss of trust

All active LondonoSolutions protocols are now expected to map back to:
- a defined offering

## Offering Categories

(Establish how thinking is formed)

#### What we deliver

- Decision taxonomies
- Prompt and reasoning templates
- Value boundaries and constraints
- Human escalation rules

#### Doctrine alignment
- Human-in-Command
- Replaceability of Models
- Decision Ownership

#### Outcome
- AI systems think the same way every time
- Decisions are explainable before automation begins

### 2. Intelligence Memory & Continuity Systems

(Preserve organizational thinking)

#### Doctrine alignment
- Memory Ownership

- Replace executive judgment

If an offering cannot pass all five stages, it does not ship.
